You can try one Video without signing up. A registered **Free Plan** includes:

- 10 summaries per month
- 5 Video Chat messages per Video
- A 10-item History

The **Pro Plan** includes unlimited summaries, Video Chat, and History. It costs $6.99 per month with monthly billing, or a $4.99 per month equivalent billed as $59.88 annually. No credit card is required for the Free Plan. [Compare plans on Pricing](/pricing).

Registered Learners can review their current plan and usage in [Plan & Billing](/account/billing). Active Pro Learners use **Manage subscription** there to open the Stripe Customer Portal for payment methods, invoices, cancellation, resumption, and billing-cadence changes.
