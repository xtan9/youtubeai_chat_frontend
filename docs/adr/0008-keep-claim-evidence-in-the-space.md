# Keep research available throughout script writing

A creator can start with a question and a few sources before there is any script. In the Space conversation, the creator and AI explore what is worth saying, draft passages and revise them together.

The AI takes initiative within that work. It compares what individual sources say, looks for relevant literature and explains which points have support, where accounts conflict and what remains open. It links findings to the source passage, timestamp or paper and keeps them accessible in the Space evidence view. The creator can draw on the same research when moving from a discussion to a draft or when selecting a passage for revision.

Keeping this material in the Space requires preserving source context across the conversation and drafts. It lets the creator return to a discovery and use it elsewhere in the script.

The evidence view groups findings by research question across sources. The source library also gives access to the points drawn from each individual resource.

An explicit request to write a passage puts the result in the editor, where the creator can edit or undo it. A request for alternatives keeps the options in the conversation until the creator selects one.

On import, each resource has its own claim assessment. Show the three to five claims most consequential to its argument first, with the total and access to every assessed claim. Supported, problematic and open categories may be empty. Distinguish finding no clear error after review from an incomplete search. Each assessment shows evidence strength and direction, AI confidence in its judgment, reasons and available source references. One click investigates that claim in the Space’s separate research conversation and retains the resulting evidence under the corresponding question.

Import organizes the resource itself: extract its key claims, locate the original passages and show references supplied by the author. An author-supplied citation can be shown before its contents are verified. Claims with no external assessment are marked pending. An explicit investigation request in chat or a click on “继续深究” starts external research for the requested question.

When research is used in a script, keep the spoken text readable and attach a discreet source marker to the relevant sentence. Opening the marker shows the supporting video timestamp or paper passage and any relevant disagreement retained from the investigation.

Highlighting text supplies context for chat. Questions and ambiguous requests default to discussion, with suggestions the creator can choose to apply. An explicit instruction to revise the selection replaces just that passage in the editor and offers one-step undo.
