We process the YouTube URL you submit, the transcript we fetch or generate from it, and the resulting summary. Here's what happens to each:

- **The URL and video details** identify the video being processed and its cached results.
- **The transcript** can be stored in our database so later requests can reuse it without fetching or transcribing the video again.
- **The summary** can also be cached for reuse, including summaries requested without a registered account. Signed-in users have a separate history linking their account to videos they have summarized.

We send video content to our AI service through a gateway to generate summaries and answers. Cached video content and your personal viewing history are separate; signing out does not delete cached transcripts or summaries.

For the full policy — including cookie use, analytics, and your access/deletion rights — see the [Privacy Policy](/privacy).
