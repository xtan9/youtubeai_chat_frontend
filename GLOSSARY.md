# Creator Spaces

Creators gather material, discuss ideas and write together with AI in a Space.

## Language

**Workspace**  
The product entry point where a creator opens and manages their Spaces.

**Space**  
A place for one creative or research effort, such as making a video, investigating a question or writing an article.

**Evidence**  
Material traced to a source that helps assess a particular claim. The AI actively seeks relevant evidence, including findings that challenge or qualify the claim, and the creator can revisit it in the Space.

**Space type**  
The kind of creative work a Space is set up for. Video creation is the first Space type.

**Script**  
The creator’s editable draft of what an audience will hear or see in a video.

**Selected passage**  
The words a creator highlights in a draft to discuss with AI. A question about “this sentence” refers to those words when the creator moves into chat.

**Writing conversation**  
The writing partner’s ongoing discussion within a Space. The creator can switch between drafts and continue the same discussion. Messages about selected passages retain the quoted text and identify the originating draft.

**Assessment confidence**  
How certain the AI is about its assessment of a specific claim in a resource. The AI can be highly confident that a cited study leaves a question unanswered while the evidence for the claim itself remains weak.

**Evidence strength**  
How strongly the available sources, weighed for quality, directness and consistency, support or challenge a specific claim. The direction of the evidence is stated alongside its strength. When relevant material could not be examined, strength is unassessed.

**Research conversation**  
The Space’s separate discussion of claims and their evidence. A creator can explicitly hand a finding to the writing partner.
